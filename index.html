Terms & Conditions

  • Employees achieving multiplier less than 0.8X are not eligible for incentive payout.
  • Incentive payout is calculated for 9 months only.
  • Maximum cap for SBU & Division is 3X.
  • Maximum cap for Zone / Region / Territory is 6X.
  • Marketing Activities maximum multiplier is 1.5X.
  • Employee should spend more than 3 months in territory during evaluation period.
  • Employee should be on payroll during incentive disbursement.
  • Payouts are annual but calculated on 9 months' performance.
